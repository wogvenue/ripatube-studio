Privacy Policy

RipaTube Studio · last updated 10 November 2026

Who uses it

RipaTube Studio is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Wogvenue, on their own computer, to manage that channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google OAuth, the tool uses the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API to upload and schedule the channel's videos, set thumbnails, captions and playlists, and read the channel's own performance statistics.

What it stores and shares

  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data about viewers or other YouTube users is collected beyond the aggregated statistics YouTube provides to the channel owner.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Wogvenue YouTube channel or by opening an issue on GitHub.
